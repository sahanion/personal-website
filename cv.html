02 / Research

Research Projects

Nano Covalent Organic Framework as Cancer Therapeutic Platform

Master’s Thesis

Indian Institute of Technology Mandi

  • Engineered surface-modified nano COFs for stimuli-responsive targeted cancer drug delivery.
  • Optimized drug loading, achieving 50% higher cancer cell death.
  • Characterized nanoparticles using SEM, TEM, DLS, and PXRD techniques.
  • Studied fate of COF nanocarriers inside a cell, tracking sub-cellular localization of diverse functionalized nano COFs.

Utilization and Assimilation of Inorganic Carbon Using Microalgae, and Production of Biomass

Bachelor’s Project

Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology

  • Designed and built a low-cost photobioreactor for efficient wastewater microalgae cultivation.
  • Isolated, identified, and optimized growth conditions for Desmodesmas intermedius from industrial effluent.
  • Developed and refined culture media formulations to maximize algal biomass productivity.
  • Conducted quantitative assays of harvested biomass, profiling protein, carbohydrate, and lipids.

Development of Electrochemical Point-of-Care Devices

Research Project

Wrig Nanosystems

  • Developed a biopolymer-based electrochemical glucose sensor strip for point-of-care glucose monitoring.
